# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Lead / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads guide technical direction, architecture decisions, and implementation quality. They help the team make sound engineering choices while keeping delivery aligned with business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Own technical direction and architecture decisions
- Guide implementation planning and technical trade-offs
- Help resolve technical dependencies and delivery risks
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and delivery confidence

### Goals
- Keep delivery technically sound and scalable
- Reduce rework and prevent architectural drift
- Support predictable execution across the delivery team

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical planning sessions
- Weekly syncs with PMs and Product leadership
- Technical risk discussions with developers and stakeholders

### How this role interacts with others
- Works closely with Developers on implementation quality and technical approach
- Collaborates with Product Managers on feasibility and trade-offs
- Supports Project Managers by clarifying delivery risks and dependencies

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define and coordinate quality strategy, test planning, and release readiness. They ensure that products meet acceptance criteria and are fit for release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality checkpoints
- Coordinate test planning, execution, and defect triage
- Establish release readiness criteria and quality gates
- Partner with Developers and Product Managers on acceptance criteria and defect prioritization

### Goals
- Improve product reliability and user confidence
- Reduce defects escaping into release
- Ensure quality is built into delivery from the start

### Typical Communication
- Test planning meetings and defect triage reviews
- Release readiness discussions with PMs and engineering leads
- Status updates to stakeholders on quality risks and mitigations

### How this role interacts with others
- Works with Developers to validate implementation quality
- Partners with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and release expectations
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing quality risks and readiness concerns

---

## Customer Success / Support Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Success or Support Leads represent the voice of the customer after delivery. They help teams understand how customers experience the product and where support or usability issues may arise.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback and recurring support trends
- Highlight user pain points that should influence product priorities
- Coordinate escalation and issue triage for customer-impacting problems
- Share service insights with Product and Delivery teams

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Ensure product changes reflect real user needs
- Reduce friction in the post-release experience

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and support trend reports
- Cross-functional discussions with Product and Delivery teams
- Stakeholder updates on customer impact and risk

### How this role interacts with others
- Provides input to Product Managers on customer value and pain points
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing dependencies and customer-impacting risks
- Helps Developers understand real-world usage issues and support scenarios

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design or UX Leads shape the user experience and ensure solutions are usable, intuitive, and aligned with customer expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Guide user experience and interaction design
- Translate customer needs and business goals into clear design direction
- Support usability testing and design validation
- Collaborate with Product Managers and Developers on implementation feasibility

### Goals
- Improve usability and customer experience
- Reduce friction in core workflows
- Ensure design decisions are grounded in user needs

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews, workshops, and usability testing sessions
- Product planning discussions and feature refinement
- Collaboration with engineering on implementation details

### How this role interacts with others
- Works with Product Managers to define user needs and experience goals
- Collaborates with Developers to ensure designs are implemented effectively
- Supports Project Managers by helping prioritize experience-related work and dependencies
